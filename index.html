About me

I am a researcher interested in the development of fundamental concepts for practical applications in engineering and medical acoustics, with a reach experience in numerical simulations and experimental acoustics. My expertise includes acoustic measurements, wave propagation, noise control, acoustic metamaterials, and signal analysis. I have designed experimental setups, led research projects and scientific teams, and supervised students.

I am currently open to postdoctoral research opportunities.
